## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |

| file→Package.Open | Untrusted .docx bytes cross into parser (ZIP + XML) |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-01 | DoS | zipio/part decompression | high | mitigate | Per-entry 128 MiB cap + total 512 MiB cap via io.LimitReader; ratio >100:1 flagged; ErrDecompressionLimit (OPC-07) |
| T-01-02 | Tampering | part-name validation | high | mitigate | Reject absolute paths, `..`, backslashes, drive letters, empty segments, non-UTF-8 → ErrUnsafePath; align with archive/zip ErrInsecurePath policy |
| T-01-03 | DoS | part enumeration | medium | mitigate | 4096 max-parts cap before any parse → ErrTooManyParts |
| T-01-04 | Info Disclosure | external relationships | medium | mitigate | Never resolve/fetch TargetMode="External"; surface in Warnings() |
| T-01-05 | Tampering | relationship graph | medium | mitigate | Monotonic rId allocator, no reuse; Validate() on save rejects dangling targets |
</threat_model>
