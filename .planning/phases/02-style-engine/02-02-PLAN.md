<objective>
Build the theme color concretization layer and numbering level resolution layer for the style engine, then wire both as post-processing steps into the 02-01 resolver's `ResolveRun` and `ResolveParagraph` entry points.

Purpose: STYLE-RESOLVE-03 — "Resolve theme colors and numbering definitions." This plan delivers the two post-processors that 02-01 deliberately left as pass-through: (a) `internal/style/theme.go` resolves `CT_Color.ThemeColor` enum values to concrete hex at resolve-time by lazily parsing `theme1.xml`'s `a:clrScheme` (read-only, D-06), handling the enum-to-element-name mismatch (dark1→dk1, hyperlink→hlink, etc. — Pitfall 3) and applying themeTint/themeShade math (Pitfall 5); (b) `internal/style/numbering.go` resolves numId+ilvl to a `CT_Lvl` by walking numbering.xml's CT_Num → CT_AbstractNum → CT_Lvl chain, applying lvlOverride (Pitfall 4 — requires extending `internal/wml/numbering.go` `CT_Num` struct with the `LvlOverride` field and new `CT_LvlOverride` / `CT_StartOverride` types), and merging the level's pPr (indentation) into the paragraph's effective pPr (D-13, bounded — no style-based numPr inheritance). Both layers surface missing-ref/unresolvable cases via warnings (D-07) with sensible defaults (absent CT_Color, no numPr contribution) — no sentinel errors for resolver misses.

Output: `internal/style/theme.go`, `internal/style/numbering.go`, `internal/style/theme_test.go`, `internal/style/numbering_test.go`, modified `internal/wml/numbering.go` (CT_Num extension), modified `internal/style/resolver.go` (post-processing wiring).
</objective>
